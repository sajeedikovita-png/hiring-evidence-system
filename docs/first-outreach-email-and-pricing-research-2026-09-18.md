# First outreach email and pilot pricing research — 18 September 2026

Status: founder working document. No message in this document has been sent. Competitor prices are a current public snapshot and should be checked again before using them in sales material.

## Recommendation

Use a clearly limited **S$350 founding feedback pilot for the first three agencies**. Keep S$500 as the later standard pilot price. Sell the pilot as a founder-supported working engagement rather than access to an unproven software account.

The price is reasonable when the pilot includes:

- 30 days of access for one live role, up to 50 candidate documents, and two named users;
- a 45-minute setup session to agree the role criteria and review process;
- a guided first evidence review using synthetic information before customer data is introduced;
- direct founder support during the pilot;
- a final 30-minute review of what helped, what created friction, and whether continuation makes commercial sense.

The later S$500 standard price is likely to feel expensive if the buyer thinks they are purchasing only a self-service trial. Several established alternatives let buyers start free. The founder support, controlled scope, client-handoff use case, and end-of-pilot review therefore need to appear beside the price.

The founder approved the clearly limited **S$350 founding feedback pilot for the first three agencies** on 18 September 2026. The S$350 payment is credited toward the first S$800 continuation term if the company continues. Keep S$500 as the later standard pilot price. Do not quietly quote different prices to similar companies without recording the reason and offer terms.

The S$800 founding continuation may be testable after the pilot demonstrates repeated use. The S$1,400 standard term will need measured customer value, stronger operational proof, and likely broader usage before a small agency will accept it comfortably.

## How to use the website in outreach

The first email should earn a short conversation. It should not try to explain the whole product.

1. Research one agency and one relevant person. Use the company website or another public business source.
2. Send an individual email from `Sajeewa | Hiring Evidence <support@hiringevidence.com>` until a founder-named domain address is available.
3. Mention one specific reason the workflow may fit that agency.
4. Link to the homepage or a short product walkthrough. Do not add several attachments to the first cold email.
5. Ask for a 15-minute workflow conversation. Do not ask them to buy in the first message.
6. After an interested reply, send the short video, synthetic sample report, and one-page pilot scope.
7. Follow up once after four or five business days, then stop unless they respond.

For individually researched outreach, keep the message personal and small in volume. Do not harvest addresses or send a bulk blast. Singapore's Spam Control Act requirements apply to unsolicited bulk commercial electronic messages, including unsubscribe facilities; the IMDA guidance should be reviewed before any campaign. Business contact information is generally outside the PDPA's scope, but that does not remove the need to use it responsibly and honour opt-outs.
