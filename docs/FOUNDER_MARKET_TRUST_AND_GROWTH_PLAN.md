# Founder market, trust, and growth plan

Status date: 11 September 2026

This plan begins after owner acceptance testing is complete. It recognises that the founder is also pursuing singing and music releases and cannot remain the permanent salesperson or day-to-day country operator.

Hiring Evidence must first prove that it solves a costly hiring-review problem. Visual polish, government guidance, and trust marks support that proof, but they do not replace a reliable product or customer results.

## Operating principle

For the first customers, the founder performs the sales conversations, demonstrations, onboarding, and feedback interviews. The purpose is to learn what buyers ask, which outcomes they value, and why they hesitate.

After the process is repeatable, appoint one independent country partner or country lead for each active market. Do not launch three markets at the same time.

Recommended order:

1. Complete and record the live owner acceptance test.
2. Run one controlled Singapore paid pilot.
3. Use warm contacts to obtain one Sri Lankan founding-partner pilot.
4. Record measurable customer outcomes and obtain permission for a reference or anonymised case study.
5. Turn the founder's successful conversation into a repeatable sales pack.
6. Appoint a Singapore sales partner and a Sri Lanka sales partner only when each market has a working lead source.
7. Complete Australian privacy, employment, security, support, and contract readiness before appointing an Australian partner.

## Founder involvement and eventual handoff

### Stage 1: founder-led validation

The founder owns the first three to five qualified conversations and at least the first two pilot onboardings. Use the scripted 15-minute conversation in `FOUNDER_ZOOM_CONVERSATION_SCRIPT.md`. Keep a record of the buyer's existing process, repeated problems, objections, desired outcomes, decision maker, budget, and follow-up date.

Limit founder sales work to two fixed blocks each week so that product work and music commitments remain manageable. Prefer short video calls and scheduled demonstrations over unplanned company visits.

### Stage 2: independent sales partners

Use two clearly separated arrangements:

- Referral partner: makes a qualified introduction and receives 10% of the first collected customer payment.
- Independent sales partner: finds, presents to, follows up with, and helps close customers; receives 20% of collected revenue for that customer's first six months.

Commission is earned only after cleared customer payment. The agreement must define refunds, cancellations, confidentiality, customer ownership, taxes, termination, approved claims, and the partner's lack of authority to change prices or promise legal compliance. A Singapore lawyer or employment adviser should review the contract before signature. A partner operating in Sri Lanka or Australia also needs a contract suitable for that country.

A software sales partner must not place jobseekers, process their job applications, or submit candidates to employers unless the necessary employment-agency requirements have been checked.

### Stage 3: country ownership

Create a country-lead role only after a market produces repeatable revenue. The initial role can remain an independent commercial arrangement. A country lead should own prospecting, demonstrations, follow-up, onboarding coordination, customer feedback, and a monthly pipeline report. Product decisions, compliance claims, contracts, pricing exceptions, and access to candidate data remain under founder/company control.

Each country partner receives the same controlled sales kit:

- one-page problem and outcome brief;
- 15-minute conversation script;
- five-minute synthetic-data demonstration;
- approved pricing and discount boundaries;
- answers to common privacy, AI, and human-control questions;
- prohibited claims and government-logo rules;
- pilot success-measure worksheet;
- follow-up email templates;
- customer access and escalation procedure.

## Signature problem-solving capability

Build and market an **Evidence Challenge Plan** as the memorable product capability.

For every job requirement, it should show:

1. the candidate claim;
2. the source-linked evidence found;
3. the evidence gap or uncertainty;
4. a targeted interview question;
5. a suggested proof or reference check;
6. the recruiter's verification status and note.

Suggested public message:

> Turn uncertain CV claims into a verification plan. Hiring Evidence connects job requirements to candidate evidence, identifies what remains unclear, and prepares the questions and proof requests needed for human review.

Suggested workspace action: `Resolve evidence gaps`.

This should reuse the current evidence, missing-evidence, verification, interview-question, human-decision, and audit records through the repository/service layer. It must never rank candidates or make the hiring decision.

## Website changes that make the value immediately visible

Prioritise the following changes after acceptance testing:

1. **Problem-first hero:** state the operational problem in the first screen: CV claims are difficult to verify consistently before interview and client submission.
2. **Visible transformation:** show one realistic before-and-after example: an unsupported CV claim becomes source evidence, a gap, an interview question, and a proof request.
3. **Interactive sample:** let a visitor explore a synthetic Evidence Challenge Plan without signing in or exposing real candidate data.
4. **Three-step workflow:** `Set job evidence criteria`, `Inspect claims and gaps`, `Record the human review`.
5. **Outcome language:** emphasise consistent review, preparation time, verification readiness, accountable records, and easier handoff. Do not publish savings figures until measured with customers.
6. **Human-made visual identity:** retain the light editorial/newspaper direction, hand-drawn recruiter illustration, generous readable type, restrained motion, and specific product examples. Avoid generic gradients, floating glass cards, decorative robots, and stock AI imagery.
7. **Trust centre:** publish plain-language pages for security, privacy, AI use, subprocessors, retention, incident contact, human control, and country-readiness status.
8. **Evidence behind claims:** replace broad claims with product screenshots, short demonstrations, assurance-test summaries, and customer-approved case studies.
9. **Clear market route:** provide `Start Singapore pilot` and `Discuss a regional pilot` actions with the limits and commercial path visible before submission.
10. **Earned marks only:** display certification logos only after formal certification and according to the issuing body's logo conditions.

## Singapore trust and certification path

There is no general Singapore Government certificate that declares an AI recruitment product legally compliant, fair, accurate, or approved for hiring. Do not market Hiring Evidence as `Singapore Government approved`, `PDPA certified`, or guaranteed compliant.

There are credible programmes that can strengthen buyer confidence when their requirements are actually met:

### 1. Data Protection Trustmark

IMDA's Data Protection Trustmark, now based on SS 714:2025, is a voluntary enterprise-wide certification assessed by appointed independent certification bodies. This is the strongest relevant medium-term trust signal because Hiring Evidence processes candidate personal data.

Preparation requires more than website copy. Complete data inventories, policies, customer and processor responsibilities, retention and deletion, access and correction handling, subprocessor management, incident response, staff practices, and evidence that controls operate consistently. Apply only when these are ready for assessment.

Official information: https://www.imda.gov.sg/how-we-can-help/data-protection-trustmark-certification

### 2. CSA Cyber Essentials

CSA's Cyber Essentials mark is a practical earlier cybersecurity milestone for organisations beginning their cybersecurity journey. The current scheme includes classical cybersecurity, cloud security, and AI-security considerations. Certification is performed through CSA-appointed certification bodies and is valid for two years.

Use it after documenting asset ownership, access control, authentication, patching, backups, endpoint and cloud protection, incident response, supplier risks, and security testing. Consider the higher Cyber Trust mark later if customer risk and procurement expectations justify it.

Official information: https://www.csa.gov.sg/our-programmes/support-for-enterprises/sg-cyber-safe-programme/cybersecurity-certification-for-organisations/cyber-essentials/certification-for-the-cyber-essentials-mark/

### 3. AI Verify and Project Moonshot aligned assurance

AI Verify and related testing resources provide governance and technical-testing methods. Using them can produce stronger evidence about defined product behaviours, but participation or test completion is not blanket government approval and does not prove that the system is risk-free or free from discrimination.

Maintain a versioned assurance report covering source accuracy, unsupported statements, prompt injection, protected-characteristic handling, human override, repeatability, failures, model changes, and limitations. Publish a careful summary and make the detailed report available during customer review.

Official information: https://www.imda.gov.sg/about-imda/emerging-technologies-and-research/artificial-intelligence

## Trust roadmap

### Before the first paid pilot

- Finish live acceptance testing and close all high-impact findings.
- Complete privacy-request, retention, deletion, incident, and subprocessor documentation.
- Prepare customer agreement, data-processing terms, candidate notice, and support process for professional review.
- Run and preserve the defined AI assurance tests.
- Publish a truthful trust centre without certification logos.

### After the first measured pilots

- Publish one permissioned or anonymised case study with measured starting point and result.
- Perform a Cyber Essentials gap assessment and obtain certification quotations.
- Perform a DPTM SS 714:2025 readiness assessment and cost review.
- Decide which certification buyers actually value before committing significant funds.

### After repeatable revenue

- Pursue the trust mark that buyers identify as most valuable.
- Add the awarded logo and exact certification scope only after confirmation.
- Reassess Cyber Trust, ISO/IEC 27001, Australian privacy readiness, and other market-specific assurance as customer size and procurement requirements grow.

## Sales-readiness gate

Marketing outreach begins only after the founder can demonstrate, using synthetic data, the complete path from job criteria through document processing, source-linked evidence, the Evidence Challenge Plan, human decision reason, audit history, privacy handling, access expiry, and company separation.

The first sales objective is not traffic. It is two qualified paid pilots with an agreed success measure and an honest end-of-pilot review.
