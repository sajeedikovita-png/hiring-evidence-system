# Premium pilot commercial proposal

Status: development direction updated on 10 September 2026. Start with specialist recruitment agencies using a limited paid pilot, three founding ongoing terms, and a disclosed standard renewal price. Prices and capacity remain hypotheses to validate with customers and measured costs. See the step-by-step development plan in `2026-09-09-premium-pilot-development-plan.md`.

The post-testing sales, regional-partner, website differentiation, founder-handoff, and Singapore trust-mark sequence is recorded in `../FOUNDER_MARKET_TRUST_AND_GROWTH_PLAN.md`.

## Commercial objective

Build a product capable of earning S$1,400 per company per month from a small customer base. This is a target to validate with buyers, not an established market price or a claim about the current app's readiness.

Keep the intended ongoing price visible from the first qualified sales conversation. Do not introduce the complete offering as a permanent S$100 subscription and then require the same customers to pay S$1,400 for substantially the same scope.

## Recommended first customer hypothesis

Specialist recruitment agencies with roughly 5–15 consultants, several concurrent professional hiring assignments, and recurring work preparing candidate evidence and client submissions. Reach the owner or operations lead who owns this workflow and budget. The important qualification is repeated work and measurable benefit, not company size alone.

Initially support an existing recruitment workflow without requiring the buyer to migrate an entire ATS. Client submissions are already offered by competitors; source accuracy, consistent criteria, review records, ease of handoff, and measured time savings must earn the additional spend.

Alternative under consideration: internal HR teams hiring regularly. Their primary workflow would be hiring-manager review and sign-off rather than client submissions. The selected segment should determine implementation priorities.

## Proposed entry offer

1. Free walkthrough using synthetic data. Explain both the pilot and ongoing price.
2. S$500 fixed-price, 30-day evaluation after the real workflow is ready. Proposed limits: one role, up to 50 candidate documents, and two named internal users. Include setup, one training session, and a closing results review. Confirm operational costs before quoting these limits.
3. Founding company access at S$800 for each of the first three manually renewed 30-day terms, followed by the disclosed S$1,400 standard price from term four. Proposed scope: five named internal users, up to ten active roles and 500 candidate document reviews per term, consistent job criteria, source-linked evidence reports, interview preparation, team review/sign-off, retrievable decision history, controlled report sharing, onboarding, and defined support hours. Planned capabilities must not be presented as current inclusions until verified.

The S$500 pilot fee is a separate evaluation purchase. If the buyer continues, each of the first three ongoing 30-day terms is S$800. The standard price is S$1,400 from term four. State the full schedule before the pilot begins. There is no automatic conversion, renewal, or charge.

Proposed subscription terms: monthly billing in advance, no compulsory annual prepayment for initial customers, clear cancellation and data-handling terms, and the agreed price protected for 12 months. Future renewal pricing must be communicated in advance under the agreement. Extra capacity or support requires a separate agreed scope; do not promise unlimited processing or unlimited personal service.

Have the final customer agreement and applicable tax treatment checked before issuing offers. No payment integration is needed to validate interest or demonstrate the product.

## Evidence needed to justify the price

- Compare the buyer's existing process with the pilot on similar work, including time spent correcting output and verifying sources.
- Record baseline volume, review time, preparation time, correction rates, and repeated use. Obtain customer agreement on what success means before work starts.
- Calculate time value using the buyer's own assumptions. For illustration only: 60 hours of usable capacity saved per month at S$50/hour equals S$3,000 of capacity value. It is not necessarily a cash saving, and it is not a measured result for this app.
- Check willingness to pay the S$800 founding price and later accept the disclosed S$1,400 standard price. Seek two or three customers who convert and continue before widening the build or spending heavily on acquisition.
- Measure hosting, processing, support, and sales costs. Three customers yield S$4,200 monthly revenue; five yield S$7,000, before expenses and owner compensation.

## Current technical status and release gates

The current checkout has access-request and invitation foundations, report UI, repository boundaries, and database/storage groundwork. Upload UI still simulates incoming rows. Workspace access, source verification, and full document-to-report processing require completion. Some fairness and reviewer labels are fixed text. This is not the advertised premium package yet.

1. Trust and access: separate public synthetic samples from authenticated company routes; require active membership; remove unsupported fairness success messages; ensure account changes clear private state. Verify server/database policies separately from browser guards.
2. Real review workflow: company-scoped job criteria, private uploads, real processing with honest failure states, source references that a reviewer can inspect, correction handling, and saved human decisions. Select processing providers and deployment settings explicitly before connecting production AI or sending live messages.
3. Team delivery: relevant agency or internal-HR handoffs, reviewer roles, retrievable amendment history, and controlled sharing. Complete privacy, retention, deletion, and support procedures. Assess client-level isolation if agencies are selected.
4. Commercial validation: run the bounded evaluation, measure outcome and costs, and offer the stated subscription only for capabilities actually available. Final marketing claims need a documented mapping to implemented behavior and relevant Singapore guidance.

No release gate establishes that the software certifies legal compliance or decides whom an employer should hire. AI assists. Human decides. Evidence explains.

## Sources informing the comparison

- [Manatal pricing](https://www.manatal.com/pricing): low-cost per-user competition means generic resume analysis alone is insufficient differentiation.
- [Recruit CRM pricing and features](https://recruitcrm.io/pricing/): candidate submissions, report generation, team roles, and audit logs already appear in competing offerings.
- [MOM interview and job-offer record guidance](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/fair-consideration-framework/must-my-company-keep-a-record-of-interviews-and-job-offer-decisions): supports the recordkeeping use case; it does not mandate buying this app.
