# First customer plan — 17 September 2026

Status: working sales plan for founder review. Outreach copy below is unsent. The prospect tracker is intentionally blank; it contains fields and rows only, not fabricated leads.

## Objective and guardrails

Over 14 days, run a focused, zero-ad-budget search for one or two qualified conversations with small specialist recruitment agencies. The immediate goal is learning and a possible paid pilot, not claiming a result before a company has measured it. The founder should own the first conversations, demonstrations, pilot setup, and end-of-pilot review.

Target agencies with roughly 5–15 consultants, several concurrent professional hiring assignments, and recurring work preparing candidate evidence or client submissions. Reach an owner, operations lead, head of recruitment, or person who controls the review workflow and budget. Do not force the product into a workflow that does not involve repeated evidence review.

Use synthetic candidate information in demonstrations. Do not request candidate documents during prospecting. Do not claim time savings, accuracy, fairness, compliance, certification, or customer outcomes until they are measured and approved for publication. Keep the product explanation precise: AI assists with organising job-related evidence; a human reviews it and records the hiring decision and reason.

Use free channels and existing relationships: personal introductions, direct email, LinkedIn messages, local agency directories, recruiter communities, and short calls where a public business number is available. Record the source and date for every contact. Respect opt-outs and local marketing rules.

## Fifteen-minute demo script

Use the prospect’s own problem to decide whether to demonstrate. Stop after any step if it does not map to their workflow.

1. **Set context — 1 minute.** “I’ll use synthetic candidate information. I want to show how job criteria, source evidence, missing proof, and a recruiter’s written review fit together. The system assists with organising evidence; it does not make the hiring decision.”
2. **Ask first — 3 minutes.** “What role are you reviewing? Who checks the CV? Where do you record evidence and questions for the client? What has to be verified before a submission?”
3. **Define job criteria — 2 minutes.** Show the role criteria and say: “These job-related criteria give each review a consistent structure.”
4. **Upload and prepare — 2 minutes.** Show a synthetic document and the preparation state: “This is a demonstration document. A real company would use its authorised workspace and its agreed data-handling process.”
5. **Inspect the evidence report — 3 minutes.** Show evidence found, evidence missing, source/page references, and suggested verification questions. Say: “The report helps the reviewer see what is supported and what still needs checking. It is not a guarantee that every claim is correct.”
6. **Record human review — 2 minutes.** Open the review controls: “The recruiter decides what to do and must record a reason. The system does not rank or choose the person.”
7. **Close — 2 minutes.** Ask: “Where would this fit or fail in your current workflow?” Then, only if they are interested: “The founding feedback pilot is S$350 one time for the first three agencies. It covers 30 days, one role, up to 50 candidate documents, and two named users. The S$350 is credited toward the first S$800 ongoing term if the company continues; the later standard pilot price is S$500. The disclosed ongoing schedule is S$800 for each of the first three 30-day terms and S$1,400 from term four. Every term requires manual agreement; there is no automatic renewal or charge. Would you like to discuss a measured pilot scope?”

Record the company, participant role, current process, strongest problem, objections, requested capabilities, decision maker, budget signal, and agreed next action. Do not record candidate information from the call.

## End-of-cycle review

Count only what the tracker supports. Review reply rate, qualified conversation rate, demo-to-follow-up rate, objections, time spent per prospect, and opt-outs. For a pilot discussion, agree the baseline before work begins: number of roles and documents, current review/preparation time, correction or verification work, who reviews, and what evidence would make the evaluation useful. A positive conversation is not a measured product outcome.
