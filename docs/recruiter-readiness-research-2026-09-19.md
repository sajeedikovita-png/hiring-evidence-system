# Hiring Evidence recruiter-readiness research

Date: 19 September 2026

## Decision

Hiring Evidence does **not** yet contain everything a recruiter expects from a complete applicant tracking system. It is much closer to a focused evidence-review and client-handoff product. That is a useful position: competing with mature ATS platforms feature-for-feature would weaken the product and delay revenue.

The product is credible for a founder-supported paid pilot when the sale is framed precisely:

> Hiring Evidence turns one agreed client brief and a batch of CVs into source-linked evidence records, missing-proof questions, and an accountable human review trail.

Before taking the first paid pilot, close the client-sharing, live onboarding, and data-operations gaps listed below. Then learn from the first agencies before building a wider recruitment suite.

## What is already strong

| Recruiter need | Current evidence | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define role-specific requirements before reviewing candidates | Job creation supports required and preferred criteria, with descriptions of observable evidence | Strong |
| Process a batch of private candidate documents | Private bulk upload, text extraction, manual text check, and report generation exist | Strong |
| Explain why a profile is relevant | The report maps every criterion to source-linked evidence, uncertainty, or missing proof | Core differentiator |
| Avoid hiding uncertainty | Reports separate evidence found, verification needed, and missing evidence | Strong |
| Prepare a useful recruiter conversation | Suggested verification questions are tied to evidence gaps | Strong, but answers are not captured yet |
| Preserve human responsibility | A recruiter must choose the outcome and enter a job-related reason | Strong |
| Inspect public professional material responsibly | Candidate confirmation is required and the recruiter supplies the job-related text and source URL | Strong boundary |
| Keep companies separated | Authentication, company-scoped repositories, private storage, and controlled access workflows exist | Strong technical foundation |
| Support a controlled trial | Pilot lifecycle, limits, support requests, admin review, and manual renewals exist | Strong pilot operations foundation |

The most defensible distinction is the connected record:

**client requirement -> candidate source -> evidence or gap -> verification question -> human decision reason**

General ATS products organize applicants, stages, communication, and scheduling. Hiring Evidence should own the quality and explainability of the recruiter-to-client submission.

## What recruiters will notice is missing

### Must close before the first paid pilot

1. **A client-ready handoff.** The product promises a stronger client conversation but does not yet give the recruiter a secure client share link or a clean PDF/print export. Add a read-only summary with source references, evidence gaps, verification questions, recruiter note, expiry, and access controls.
2. **One consistent live offer and onboarding path.** The production site inspected on 19 September still displayed the S$500 pilot while the current source contains the S$350 founding offer. The detailed walkthrough also mentions S$500. Deploy one agreed price and scope everywhere, then verify request submission, invitation delivery, password setup, recovery, and support mailbox delivery with a fresh external inbox.
3. **Executable privacy operations.** The workspace records access, correction, deletion, and withdrawal requests, but its own UI states that recording a request does not perform the underlying action. Before real candidate data, define retention, complete actual export/correction/deletion procedures, and publish the DPA, subprocessors/transfers, incident contact, and customer responsibilities.

### Build with the first customers

1. **Interview verification record.** Let a recruiter record the answer, evidence, interviewer, date, and outcome beside each suggested question. A generated question without its follow-up result leaves the evidence trail unfinished.
2. **Clear workflow ownership.** Add assigned reviewer, simple statuses, next action, due date, and an activity history. Current report profiles can show `Not recorded` for the assigned recruiter.
3. **Editable team notes and structured feedback.** Current recruiter notes are report data rather than a clear collaborative note workflow. Add criterion-linked notes, reviewer attribution, and a light scorecard or verification form.
4. **Basic interoperability.** Start with CSV import/export and a documented handoff format. Add API/webhook or one ATS integration only after a paying customer confirms which system matters.
5. **Candidate identity hygiene.** Add duplicate detection or a deliberate merge/review step for repeated CVs and candidates.
6. **Simple communication handoffs.** Copyable email templates and calendar links may be enough initially. Native email, SMS, and scheduling can wait until usage proves they are necessary.

### Deliberately postpone

- Job-board distribution and sourcing databases
- Career-site builder and candidate portal
- Offer letters, e-signatures, and employee onboarding
- Native video interviewing, SMS, and a mobile app
- Complex automation and advanced recruitment analytics

Those areas are expensive and are already mature in existing ATS products. Hiring Evidence should integrate with them rather than reproduce them before revenue.

## Current market baseline

## Product position to use

Avoid presenting Hiring Evidence as a cheaper version of Workable, Zoho, Breezy, or Greenhouse. That comparison makes the missing ATS features the centre of the sale.

Use this position instead:

> Your ATS tells you where a candidate is. Hiring Evidence shows the client why the profile is relevant, where the proof came from, and what still needs verification before a person decides.

For the founding pilot, sell the outcome rather than software access alone:

- one real role and its agreed requirements;
- up to 50 candidate documents;
- structured evidence reports;
- a client-ready shortlist handoff;
- a guided review with the founder;
- a closing review that records what saved time and what must improve.

## Recommended sequence

1. Align and deploy one pilot price, scope, and video narration.
2. Add the secure client share/export.
3. Run the complete fresh-customer onboarding and support-delivery test.
4. Complete the real-data privacy and commercial pack.
5. Start founder-led outreach to a small number of specialist agencies.
6. Build interview capture, ownership, notes, and interoperability from observed pilot use.

The product does not need every recruiter feature before it can earn revenue. It needs one valuable outcome that is complete, trustworthy, and easy to show to a client.
