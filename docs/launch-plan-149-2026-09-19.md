# Hiring Evidence — revised launch plan

Prepared 19 September 2026 at 15:11 Singapore time. Target checkpoint: 16:00 today.

## Agreed offer to implement

- S$149 per company per monthly access term, including the first paid pilot term.
- Two named users, two active roles, 50 new candidate documents per term.
- One initial 30-minute setup session and email support. No compulsory setup charge.
- No compulsory annual contract or automatic charge. Renewal remains manually agreed.
- First five paying companies receive this price for 12 months within the stated scope. Track eligibility and price expiry explicitly.
- Company-specific development requires a separate written scope and quote. It is not included in the subscription.
- Optional free evaluation: one role and up to three fictional or appropriately authorized CVs, guided by the founder. Do not promise an automated free trial until its limits are implemented.
- Treat S$149 as an initial offer to validate; measure provider costs, hosting, support time, usage, and renewals. Additional capacity requires agreement; no hidden overage charges.
- Define monthly term dates consistently before release: use the existing 30-day access period and describe it explicitly as S$149 per 30-day term wherever contractual precision matters.

## Positioning

Already using recruitment software? Keep it. See what a source-linked evidence report adds to your next client submission.

Hiring Evidence helps recruiters connect job requirements to candidate evidence, identify missing proof, prepare verification questions, and record their own decision reasons. Existing ATS software can continue handling sourcing, communication, scheduling, and offers. Do not imply an integration exists until implemented.

## Execution order and acceptance criteria

### 1. Pricing and entitlement consistency — first implementation

Update the landing page, request form, terms, pilot-access screen, administrative approvals, lifecycle types and mapping, database functions, new plan version, limits, and fixtures/tests. Include two roles, 50 documents, and two users in both UI and server enforcement. Remove the previous pilot credit and staged S$800/S$1,400 offer from new sales paths.

Preserve existing customer agreements and historical records. Do not rewrite old invoices or silently reduce existing access. New pricing must be versioned; any existing account migration must be explicit and reviewed.

Acceptance: one new customer sees the same price and scope from public offer through approval and renewal. Server rejects excess usage. Existing plan records still load correctly. The first-five-company offer is auditable, with expiry and renewal handling defined.

### 2. Client-ready evidence handoff — highest product priority

Build an authenticated or securely invited, read-only client report containing role criteria, supporting evidence references, gaps, questions, recruiter reason, and preparation date. Preview before sharing; exclude internal notes and private documents by default. Include expiry, revocation, company scoping, and tests for unauthorised access. Add a print-friendly view; dedicated PDF export can follow if it cannot be completed and verified today.

Acceptance: the intended recipient can inspect the approved summary; another company cannot; expired and revoked access fail; the recruiter can preview exactly what is shared. Never turn existing private report URLs into unrestricted public links.

### 3. Fresh customer and data-operation proof

Test request, invitation delivery, account setup, login, recovery, role creation, CV upload, report generation, human decision persistence, support submission and response delivery. Use fictional data for QA.

Confirm real-data retention, access/export, correction, withdrawal, deletion and storage removal procedures; publish accurate contact, processing terms and subprocessor information. A privacy request marked resolved is not evidence that the underlying data action occurred.

Acceptance: record observable results and remaining blockers. Passing code tests alone is not launch proof.

### 4. Demonstrations and narration

Update BOTH the short homepage video and the detailed interactive walkthrough. Inventory every price/scope mention in scripts, generated audio, on-screen labels, captions, screenshots, and call-to-action slides.

Use the approved voice and existing balanced settings. Regenerate only narration whose wording changes. Capture final UI only after pricing and sharing screens settle. Show the client handoff only after it works. Keep cursor highlights, selective large captions, pause/resume, one playback per slide, and stable screen holds; prevent back-and-forth slide repetition.

Acceptance: price, scope and renewal language agree with the site; narration matches the visible action; all chapters use the approved voice; transitions, pause/resume and loudness are checked. Update media URLs/cache versions and verify the deployed result.

If revised media is not ready, withhold obsolete pricing sections or the affected video rather than publish contradictory offers. Keep working media backups.

### 5. Marketing and sales materials

Update the outreach draft, demo page, sample report invitation, FAQ, pilot scope document and founder call script. Lead with the recruiter-to-client evidence problem. Offer the small guided evaluation, then S$149 access. Do not claim guaranteed hiring outcomes, unique customization, or unsupported integrations.

Prepare drafts only; sending outreach needs the user's explicit instruction. Define one prospect segment: small specialist recruitment agencies that already source candidates but need clearer client submissions.

Acceptance: every asset uses the same scope and price, with a clear next action and no obsolete credit/renewal claims.

### 6. Improvements guided by first paying customers

- Interview answers and verification outcomes tied to criteria, reviewer and date.
- Assigned reviewer, next action, due date and simple status.
- Attributed team notes and structured feedback.
- CSV import/export and duplicate review.
- Company-specific feature controls enforced on the server, administrator enable/disable controls, audit history, isolation tests, and per-company settings. This is not available today; do not advertise it as immediately self-service.
- One proven ATS integration after customers identify the actual system they use.

Postpone broad sourcing, job boards, offers, payroll, native video interviewing and a mobile application.
