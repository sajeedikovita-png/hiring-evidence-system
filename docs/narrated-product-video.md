## Positioning for small companies

Headline: **Review candidate evidence. Know what to ask next.**

Supporting line: **A focused hiring review workspace for small teams: organise CV evidence against your role requirements, make gaps visible, and record your team's decision reasons.**

Primary call to action: **Watch the walkthrough.** Secondary: **Discuss a one-role pilot.** Do not publish a watch link before the video exists.

Audience hypothesis: a founder or small hiring team actively reviewing a batch of applications for a skilled role, with review notes spread across files and conversations. Do not assume all new companies need paid hiring software. Companies making an occasional hire with few applicants may be adequately served by a spreadsheet.

| Alternative | When it makes sense | Reason to evaluate Hiring Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| CVs and a spreadsheet | Low volume; team willing to maintain its own review template | A dedicated workflow keeps role criteria, evidence states, follow-up questions, and recorded human reasons connected. Show this directly; do not claim measured speed gains. |
| General AI chat | Flexible drafting and ad hoc summaries | A purpose-built review structure and persisted candidate records reduce the need to design a process from a blank conversation. This is a workflow comparison, not a claim that chat products lack storage, collaboration, or configurable safeguards. |
| Full applicant-tracking system | Job advertising, sourcing, pipeline management, scheduling, and wider hiring operations | Evaluate our focused evidence-review workflow if that is the unresolved problem. No ATS integration or feature superiority is promised. |

Competitor reality checked 17 September 2026: [Workable offers AI screening](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/23685011706775-Using-the-Screening-Assistant-AI-powered), and [Breezy offers candidate scorecards](https://help.breezy.hr/en/articles/5260339-creating-and-editing-candidate-scorecards). Structured review and AI alone are not unique claims. Our positioning is a fit hypothesis to validate in customer conversations, not evidence of market superiority.

Commercial caution: S$500 for the pilot and the disclosed ongoing prices may be difficult for a new business hiring infrequently. Do not advertise cheapest, affordable for everyone, or proven return on investment. Target repeated review work and test willingness to pay before changing public pricing.

Email opening draft: “Hiring for a role and keeping candidate notes in several places? Pilot proof to collect: time spent preparing reviews, whether another reviewer can locate the supporting evidence, unresolved questions identified, corrections required, and whether the team uses the workflow again. ## Detailed walkthrough chapters

1. Company access and sign-in: approval, individual accounts, existing-account recovery, no credential footage.
2. Role setup: title, required criteria, and what makes a criterion job-related.
3. Candidate uploads: accepted files, batch results, extraction checks, and how to handle a failed or incomplete file. Show actual current limits rather than inventing limits.
4. Analysis: explicit provider acknowledgement, pending state, completion, and useful next action.
5. Evidence review: statuses, source inspection, verification questions, and limitations. Show an actual source reference where available.
6. Optional professional evidence: candidate-confirmed source, human verification, and no claim of independent background checking.
7. Human decision: reason requirement, recorded decision state, and persistence. Use a clearly marked fictional training record if demonstrating a saved decision.
8. Support: guide, reporting a problem, and ticket status. Do not promise an unagreed response time.
9. Pilot scope and next steps: all disclosed pricing, manual agreement, and customer questions.
