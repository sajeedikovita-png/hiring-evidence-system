# Hiring Evidence founder Zoom conversation

Keep this page beside the Zoom window. Speak slowly and pause after each question. The purpose is to understand the company first and demonstrate the product only when its problem matches.

## Before the call

- Open the Hiring Evidence homepage, Jobs page, synthetic candidate upload, and sample evidence report.
- Use synthetic candidate information in every demonstration.
- Confirm that the person is responsible for recruitment, HR, or company hiring decisions.
- Plan for 15 to 20 minutes.

## 1. Opening — about one minute

> Thank you for meeting me. My name is Sajeewa, and I am the founder of Hiring Evidence.
>
> I am building it for companies that want a clearer way to review candidate evidence and record accountable hiring decisions.
>
> I would first like to understand how your team works today. Then, if it is relevant, I can show you a short demonstration. This conversation should take about 15 minutes.

## 2. Understand their process — about five minutes

Ask one question, listen, and take a short note before asking the next.

> How do you review CVs today?

> When several people review a candidate, where do you record the evidence and comments?

> What part of candidate review takes the most time?

> Do hiring managers or clients ask why a candidate progressed or did not progress?

> What would a new system need to prove before your team trusted it?

## 3. Repeat the problem — about one minute

Use their own words.

> Thank you. What I understand is that your main difficulty is [repeat their problem]. Have I understood that correctly?

If they say no, let them correct it. If their problem does not match the product, do not force the demonstration.

## 4. Product explanation — about one minute

> Hiring Evidence connects the company’s job criteria to evidence found in candidate documents. It shows the source for each finding, identifies missing information and records what still needs verification.
>
> AI assists with organising the evidence. It does not make the final hiring decision. A person reviews the information and must record a reason for the decision.

## 5. Demonstration — about five minutes

Show only this sequence:

1. **Job criteria**

   > First, the recruiter defines the job-related criteria. These criteria become the structure for every candidate review.

2. **Private candidate document**

   > The recruiter uploads the candidate document into the company workspace. For this demonstration, I am using synthetic information.

3. **Evidence report**

   > The report separates evidence found, missing evidence and information that needs verification. The reviewer can return to the source reference instead of accepting an unsupported summary.

4. **Human decision**

   > The system does not decide whom to hire. The recruiter records the decision and must provide a written reason.

5. **History and accountability**

   > The company keeps a clearer record of what was reviewed, what required verification and who recorded the decision.

It includes two active roles, up to 50 new candidate documents per term, two named users, one initial 30-minute setup session and email support. There is no compulsory setup fee or annual contract. Each renewal is agreed manually; there is no automatic charge.
>

>
> Keep your existing recruiting software. We can first demonstrate one role with three fictional or appropriately authorized CVs so you can judge whether the evidence review helps your client conversation. This is a guided evaluation, not an automated trial or an existing ATS integration.

## 7. Closing question

> From what you have seen, would this help with the problem you described?

Wait for the answer. Then ask:

> What concern would stop your company from trying the pilot?

If they are interested:

> Would you like me to send the one-page pilot scope and arrange a start date?

If they are not ready:

> That is completely fine. May I send you a short summary and contact you once after you have discussed it internally?

## Short answers to common questions

### Does AI choose the candidate?

> No. AI helps organise evidence. A human reviewer verifies the information and records the hiring decision and reason.

### Is it certified or approved by the Singapore Government?

> No. The product is designed around published Singapore fair-hiring, data-protection and responsible-AI guidance. It is not government-certified, and each company remains responsible for its own obligations.

### Can another company see our candidates?

> Company workspaces and private candidate documents are access-controlled. Users are limited to their authorised company workspace.

### Can you guarantee the AI is correct?

> No. The report keeps source references visible, identifies missing evidence and requires human verification. We do not present an AI draft as a final hiring decision.

### Why is the pilot paid?

> The pilot includes a controlled workspace, setup support and a structured evidence review. The fee helps both sides treat it as a serious operational test.

### Will the price rise automatically?

> There is no automatic renewal or charge. Eligible founding companies have a written 12-month price protection period. Later pricing and any additional scope must be agreed before renewal.

### Can we have a free trial?

> The first 15-minute role conversation and synthetic walkthrough are free. The paid pilot begins only after the company understands the workflow and agrees the scope.

## After the call

Record:

- the company and participant role;
- their current process;
- the strongest problem they described;
- objections and requested capabilities;
- whether they requested a proposal, follow-up or no further contact;
- the agreed next action and date.

Do not record candidate information from a discovery call.
