## ONE-PAGER (attach, or paste as the body of a warm email)

### Hire with evidence, not guesswork.

An AI-assisted candidate evidence review for hiring teams who need decisions they can **defend**.
**AI assists. Human decides. Evidence explains.**

**The problem.** Hiring teams drown in resumes — and most "AI screeners" are black boxes that auto-reject candidates with no explanation. That's a fast-growing legal and reputational risk, and you can't defend a decision you can't explain.

**What it does — the Candidate Evidence Report.** For each candidate, against *your* job's real criteria:
- Evidence found, evidence missing, and what to verify in the interview
- Suggested job-related interview questions
- A fairness check on decision wording
- A required human decision with a written reason → logged to an audit trail

**What it is NOT.** Not a black-box ranking system. Not an auto-rejecter. Not an ATS replacement. It organizes evidence so a human decides faster and more defensibly.

**Who it's for.** Recruiters, talent teams, and founders hiring for skilled roles who screen more candidates than they can read well.

**Where it is.** Working pilot — onboarding a small number of design-partner companies now.

**See it:** 2-minute demo → [LOOM LINK]. Want a pilot on one of your open roles? Reply and I'll set it up.

*AI-assisted analysis. Human review is required before making any hiring decision.*

## FINDING THE 20 (prospect list — Monday's job)

1. **MOM EA Directory** — https://service2.mom.gov.sg/eadirectory/ — every licensed
   agency in Singapore. Target: small/mid agencies placing professional/tech roles.
   Skip the giants (Michael Page, Randstad — procurement is too slow).
2. **LinkedIn** — search "recruitment agency Singapore IT" → open agency page →
   find the founder / director. In a small agency the OWNER decides and answers
   their own email.
3. **Their website** — named email + one personal fact for the opening line.
4. **Spreadsheet columns:** Agency | Person + role | Email | LinkedIn |
   "Why them" (one fact) | Status.
5. **Warm intros beat everything** — before cold-emailing, list who you already
   know (husband's network, friends). One intro > 20 cold emails.

## THE TOUCH SEQUENCE (per prospect)

- **Tue:** Email #1 (Email 3 design-partner version, personalized, Loom link)
- **Wed/Thu:** LinkedIn connect + 1-line note referencing the email
- **+3–4 days:** Follow-up email, 2 lines ("Bumping this — 2-min demo here.")
  Half of replies come from the follow-up.
- **Then stop.** Mark no-response, move on. Never chase a third time.
- **The ask is always small:** "Can I run ONE of your live roles through it,
  free, and show you the reports?" — never "buy my product."

## HOW TO ACTUALLY SEND (so it doesn't stall again)

- **List:** 10–20 named people this week. Recruiters, TA leads, or founders at small/mid companies hiring for skilled roles. LinkedIn + company careers pages → find the name.
- **Personalize line 1** (the `[reason]` / `[role]`): "saw you're hiring 3 frontend devs," "you run TA at [Company]," etc. One real sentence lifts reply rates a lot.
- **Send Tue–Thu, morning** their timezone. Plain text, no images, no tracking pixels.
- **Follow up once**, 3–4 days later: "Bumping this — 2-min demo here, worth a look?"
- **Goal for week 1:** not sales — just **replies and 1–2 pilot conversations.**

## HONEST-SELLING GUARDRAIL

When a pilot company gives you a real role and real CVs, you must actually deliver real evidence reports for them. For the first pilots that's fine to run assisted / partly by hand behind the scenes ("do things that don't scale") — just never promise fully-automated, instant, self-serve until it's built. The demo shows the workflow; the pilot delivers the outcome. Keep that line clean and you stay honest.
